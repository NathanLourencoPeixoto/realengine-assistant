--- pt
```diagram keys
Ctrl+S: Salvar | Ctrl+O: Abrir nível | Ctrl+P: Abrir asset | Ctrl+Espaço: Conteúdo | Alt+P: Jogar | F10: Debug Viewer | F1: Assistente | Shift+2: Landscape | Shift+3: Foliage | F2: Renomear
```
- **Viewport:** W/E/R mover, girar, escalar; Q selecionar; F focar; End no chão; G vista de jogo; F11 tela cheia.
- **Edição:** Ctrl+Z/Y, Ctrl+D, Shift+D, Ctrl+C/V, Delete, H/Ctrl+H, F2 renomear.
- **Arquivos:** Ctrl+S, Ctrl+Shift+S, Ctrl+O, Ctrl+N, Ctrl+P (abrir asset), Ctrl+Espaço (Gaveta de Conteúdo).
- **Jogo:** Alt+P jogar, Esc parar, F8 possuir/ejetar, Shift+F1 soltar o mouse.
- **Modos:** Shift+2 Landscape, Shift+3 Foliage; F10 Debug Viewer; F1 este assistente.
--- en
```diagram keys
Ctrl+S: Save | Ctrl+O: Open level | Ctrl+P: Open asset | Ctrl+Space: Content | Alt+P: Play | F10: Debug Viewer | F1: Assistant | Shift+2: Landscape | Shift+3: Foliage | F2: Rename
```
- **Viewport:** W/E/R move, rotate, scale; Q select; F frame; End to the floor; G game view; F11 fullscreen.
- **Editing:** Ctrl+Z/Y, Ctrl+D, Shift+D, Ctrl+C/V, Delete, H/Ctrl+H, F2 rename.
- **Files:** Ctrl+S, Ctrl+Shift+S, Ctrl+O, Ctrl+N, Ctrl+P (open asset), Ctrl+Space (Content Drawer).
- **Game:** Alt+P play, Esc stop, F8 possess/eject, Shift+F1 free the mouse.
- **Modes:** Shift+2 Landscape, Shift+3 Foliage; F10 Debug Viewer; F1 this assistant.
