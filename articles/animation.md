--- pt
- Uma **Skeletal Mesh** vem de uma importação com armature (veja [Importar](article:import-tlender)) ou do Mannequin da engine.
- Animações prontas: **Ferramentas > Animation Packs**. [Mostrar onde](show:Tools>Animation Packs)
- O **Anim Blueprint** decide qual animação toca: máquina de estados, blend 1D/2D, Montages, Aim Offset, Foot IK e **Motion Matching**.
- Para tocar uma animação por código: `PlayAnimation(...)` ou uma Montage com `PlayMontage(...)`.
--- en
- A **Skeletal Mesh** comes from an import with an armature (see [Import](article:import-tlender)) or from the engine's Mannequin.
- Ready-made animations: **Tools > Animation Packs**. [Show me where](show:Tools>Animation Packs)
- The **Anim Blueprint** picks what plays: a state machine, 1D/2D blends, Montages, Aim Offset, Foot IK and **Motion Matching**.
- To play one from code: `PlayAnimation(...)` or a Montage with `PlayMontage(...)`.
