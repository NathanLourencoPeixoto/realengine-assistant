--- pt
```diagram keys
Ctrl+Espaço: Gaveta | Ctrl+P: Buscar asset | Duplo clique: Abrir | Arrastar: Colocar no nível
```
- **Ctrl+Espaço** abre a Gaveta de Conteúdo (some ao clicar fora). [Abrir](open:content)
- Para deixar fixo: **Janela > Content Browser (docked)**.
- O botão **Adicionar** cria assets: pasta, nível, material, Blueprint, script, som...
- **Duplo clique** abre o asset no seu editor; **arraste** para o viewport para colocar.
- **Ctrl+P** busca qualquer asset pelo nome.
--- en
```diagram keys
Ctrl+Space: Drawer | Ctrl+P: Find an asset | Double-click: Open | Drag: Place in the level
```
- **Ctrl+Space** opens the Content Drawer (it hides when you click elsewhere). [Open it](open:content)
- To keep it: **Window > Content Browser (docked)**.
- The **Add** button makes assets: folder, level, material, Blueprint, script, sound...
- **Double-click** opens an asset in its editor; **drag** it into the viewport to place it.
- **Ctrl+P** finds any asset by name.
