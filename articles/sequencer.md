--- pt
```diagram steps
Adicionar > Level Sequence | Trilhas (câmera, atores, som) | Keyframes na linha do tempo | Play Sequence no jogo
```
1. Navegador: **Adicionar > Level Sequence**; duplo clique abre a linha do tempo.
2. Adicione trilhas: transform, visibilidade, luz, FOV, animação, cortes de câmera, eventos e áudio.
3. Toque no jogo com o nó **Play Sequence** (e pare com **Stop Sequence**).
--- en
```diagram steps
Add > Level Sequence | Tracks (camera, actors, sound) | Keyframes on the timeline | Play Sequence in the game
```
1. Browser: **Add > Level Sequence**; double-click opens its timeline.
2. Add tracks: transform, visibility, light, FOV, animation, camera cuts, events and audio.
3. Play it in the game with the **Play Sequence** node (and stop it with **Stop Sequence**).
