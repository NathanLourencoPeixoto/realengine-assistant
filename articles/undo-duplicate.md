--- pt
```diagram keys
Ctrl+Z: Desfazer | Ctrl+Y: Refazer | Ctrl+D: Duplicar | Shift+D: Duplicar e mover | Ctrl+C: Copiar | Ctrl+V: Colar | Delete: Apagar | H: Esconder
```
- **Ctrl+Z** desfaz, **Ctrl+Y** refaz.
- **Ctrl+D** duplica; **Shift+D** duplica e já move com o mouse.
- **Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V** copia e cola; **Delete** apaga.
- **H** esconde no editor (o jogo ainda mostra); **Ctrl+H** mostra todos.
--- en
```diagram keys
Ctrl+Z: Undo | Ctrl+Y: Redo | Ctrl+D: Duplicate | Shift+D: Duplicate and move | Ctrl+C: Copy | Ctrl+V: Paste | Delete: Delete | H: Hide
```
- **Ctrl+Z** undoes, **Ctrl+Y** redoes.
- **Ctrl+D** duplicates; **Shift+D** duplicates and moves with the mouse.
- **Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V** copy and paste; **Delete** deletes.
- **H** hides in the editor (the game still shows it); **Ctrl+H** shows all.
