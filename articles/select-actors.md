--- pt
```diagram keys
Ctrl+clique: Alternar um | Shift+arrastar: Somar | Ctrl+A: Tudo | Ctrl+I: Inverter | Esc: Limpar
```
- **Ctrl+clique** alterna um; **Shift** soma ao arrastar uma caixa no viewport ou no Outliner; **Ctrl** tira.
- **Ctrl+A** seleciona tudo; **Ctrl+I** inverte; **Esc** limpa. [Mostrar onde](show:Select)
- Com vários selecionados, mover/girar/escalar e o Details valem para todos.
--- en
```diagram keys
Ctrl+click: Toggle one | Shift+drag: Add | Ctrl+A: All | Ctrl+I: Invert | Esc: Clear
```
- **Ctrl+click** toggles one; **Shift** adds while dragging a box in the viewport or the Outliner; **Ctrl** removes.
- **Ctrl+A** selects all; **Ctrl+I** inverts; **Esc** clears. [Show me where](show:Select)
- With several selected, move/rotate/scale and Details apply to all.
