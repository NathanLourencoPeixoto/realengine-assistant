--- pt
- Ligue **Use Steam** nas Configurações do Projeto e ponha o `steam_api64.dll` do Steamworks SDK ao lado do `.rproject`. [Abrir](open:project)
- Nós: conquistas (Unlock, Progress), stats, **Upload / Download Leaderboard**, overlay, convites, presença, DLC, idioma.
- Os saves podem ir para a Steam Cloud. Sem Steam, os nós não fazem nada (o jogo roda igual).
--- en
- Turn on **Use Steam** in Project Settings and put the Steamworks SDK's `steam_api64.dll` next to the `.rproject`. [Open them](open:project)
- Nodes: achievements (Unlock, Progress), stats, **Upload / Download Leaderboard**, overlay, invites, presence, DLC, language.
- Saves can go to the Steam Cloud. Without Steam, the nodes do nothing (the game runs the same).
