--- pt
- Mixer próprio (WASAPI) com vozes reais e virtuais e streaming das Sound Waves.
- **Attenuation:** como o som cai com a distância; **Audio Volume:** reverb e zonas.
- **Sound Class / Sound Mix:** grupos de volume (música, efeitos) e mixagens (abaixar a música no diálogo).
- **Concurrency** limita quantos tocam juntos; **Submixes** com efeitos; **Sound Cues** combinam sons.
- Estéreo com binaural; **5.1 / 7.1** nativo quando o dispositivo tem.
--- en
- An own mixer (WASAPI) with real and virtual voices and streamed Sound Waves.
- **Attenuation:** how sound falls off with distance; **Audio Volume:** reverb and zones.
- **Sound Class / Sound Mix:** volume groups (music, effects) and mixes (lower the music during dialogue).
- **Concurrency** limits how many play together; **Submixes** with effects; **Sound Cues** combine sounds.
- Stereo with binaural; native **5.1 / 7.1** when the device has it.
